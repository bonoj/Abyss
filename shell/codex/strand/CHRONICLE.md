## Origin and intention

Strand is Abyss's ancient temperate world inside the existing Hermeticon: weathered stone, moss, ferns, exposed roots, mist, and eventually physically meaningful geology and living populations. The world should be composed from interacting systems rather than detached decorative props. Design references include Thronefall, Sable, Chants of Sennaar, Journey and the Stone & Root gatehouse study; avoid generic voxel blocks, arbitrary floating chips, sprites and a generic low-poly fantasy filter. A playable world for the children around Christmas is an aspiration, not an implemented game.

**Starting machinery:** reuse Arcana's Hermeticon, signed-volume terrain, atlas, chunk extraction, surface query, equatorial treatment, meteor/impact/debris/material-survey systems and Shell host. Strand remains opt-in at [`?world=strand`](https://bonoj.github.io/Abyss/shell/?world=strand); protect Genesis, Entropy, Europa and Wanderer. The source already separates world composition, Arcana infrastructure and visual treatments.

## Decisions and invariants

1. **Physics architecture is shared; resolution depends on the participating materials.** All registered bodies get one authoritative integrator. Never patch perpetual sliding with global velocity damping or freeze unsupported structures.
2. **Terrain owns geometry and geological identity.** Signed occupancy is not metric distance. Query `materialAt` and preserve conditional lineage; translate world/terrain coordinates exactly once. Use the actual extracted surface or verified projection for contact depth.
3. **Stone baseline, future heterogeneity.** Initial Strand terrain resolves as stone regardless of underlying atlas classifications. Material fields and provenance remain available for future soil, moss, ice, roots, fracture, deposition and erosion.
4. **Scale is first-class.** Thousands of rigid bricks require spatial broad phase, contact persistence and sleeping; 25k BBs require batched particle-specific kernels and typed buffers, not 25k heavyweight rigid bodies. Benchmark rather than assert performance.
5. **World ownership is explicit.** Solver emits material effects; terrain/geology/population systems commit deformation, compaction, damage and ownership changes after stepping. No hidden mutations during contact queries.
6. **Visual grammar remains independent.** Preserve Stone & Root geometry and Hermeticon framing; no sprites or substitute flat terrain in Strand. A planar test fixture is fine for deterministic headless validation.
7. **Protect existing worlds and publish honestly.** Source commit, Pages workflow, public build and browser runtime are separate verification stages. Use small reversible commits and preserve rollback.

## 2026-11-03T21:35:06Z — Approved design: targeted meteor, first-hit material response and local blast

**Human's analysis and approval.** The human proposed selecting ☄️, then tapping terrain to summon a meteor from orbit at the selected point. The human identified a critical interception case: tapping an object resting on terrain must cause the meteor to strike **that object first**, with a portion of the impact transmitted into the terrain, attenuated and spread spatially. The human asked whether spherical falloff should use inverse square, then explicitly approved a **softened, finite-radius** attenuation model and required terrain deformation to **remain local for performance**. Existing terrain deformation stays; terrain-fragment spawning is deferred until the terrain workstream. This entry records the agreed design, **not an implementation or runtime result**.

**Interaction and first-hit geometry.** ☄️ toggles armed targeting. A valid single-pointer tap casts a camera ray against collidable masonry and the existing terrain surface, choosing the nearest intersection, not automatically the ground below the tap. Drag/orbit, pinch and HUD taps must not cast; invalid intersections are no-ops. A successful cast targets the selected surface and descends along the meteor trajectory. On arrival, verify the actual first collider along that trajectory (or document and constrain a preselected collision target); don't allow a brick behind another object to receive a direct strike. Keep repeated casts possible while armed; disarm by toggling ☄️ or Escape. Preserve other worlds' meteor behavior and the established Cinderfall animation.

**Attenuation proposal approved in principle.** For radial distance r from impact, core radius a>0 and finite blast radius R>a, use f(r)=[1/(1+(r/a)^2)]*[1-(r/R)^2]^2 for 0<=r<R, otherwise zero. This is a smooth finite-support engineering kernel, not a calibrated physical shock-wave law; inverse-square spreading motivates its tail, not an exact conservation claim. Use 3D distances for body impulses, with direction from contact center toward body/contact; resolve r≈0 robustly via contact normal or incoming trajectory. Radius, core and total impulse must be explicit configurable parameters. Never scan every terrain voxel: query only impacted chunks/cells within the bounded footprint.

**Separate direct collision from blast.** The first-hit body receives a collision impulse along the incoming trajectory at its contact point, including torque and inverse-mass/inertia response. Stone, wood and ice retain their existing distinct mass, friction and restitution; begin with common blast impulse law and add material-specific transmission/absorption coefficients only as explicit, testable parameters. A separate radial disturbance gives nearby bodies attenuated impulses and wakes sleeping recipients. Momentum transferred to the first-hit body must not also be counted in full as terrain momentum; budget incoming impulse into body motion, transmitted support load, and other outgoing/dissipated channels. Energy and momentum accounting should distinguish an illustrative radial blast source from collision impulse; do not assert strict physical conservation for an externally driven terrain-deforming effect without tracking the source and sink.

**Local terrain deformation invariant.** Preserve the existing terrain.impact and its chunk-local rebuild/mutation semantics; bare terrain receives the normal concentrated crater. If a resting object intercepts the strike, reduce and spatially broaden terrain coupling according to the object's material response and contact/support footprint. Current terrain.impact accepts a scalar magnitude and center, not a distributed pressure field: v1 may use one effective center and attenuated magnitude within its existing local footprint; do not create multiple global crater calls or trigger a full terrain rebuild. Any later multi-contact footprint must aggregate bounded local contributions, mutate only affected cells/chunks, and rebuild each dirty chunk once. Deformation radius is finite and separately bounded from the body blast radius. No terrain fragments, material conversion, or geological provenance rewrites in this phase.

**Execution architecture.** Reuse Athanor pointer tap-vs-drag handling and view camera, Strand's surface query and world.castMeteor, Arcana Cinderfall explicit target extension, existing impact dispatcher and Strand brick-rain/stone-contacts. Add an optional Strand rigid-body receiver without changing Genesis/Entropy/Europa behavior. Queue impact impulses at the next fixed 120 Hz physics boundary, before integration; wake bodies affected by direct/radial impulse and by loss of terrain support. Resolve first-hit geometry with explicit collider/terrain ray intersections and stable coordinates. Expose Eye impact telemetry: hit kind/body/material, location, direct and radial impulse, ground transmission, radius/core, affected bodies by material, wake events, terrain dirty chunks, step cost, and subsequent energy. Avoid O(N²) body searches and unbounded terrain queries.

**Validation gates.** Bare ground, single resting stone/wood/ice, off-center strike and torque, overlapping/occluded bodies, nearby sleeping stack, out-of-radius untouched bodies, repeat strikes, misses, and touch gestures. Check that no full impulse is duplicated on body and ground; measure affected chunks and rebuild time, awake/sleeping transitions, kinetic energy and solver cost. ### Moss representation: hybrid biological population and surface rendering

**Core decision:** one small biological colony model drives two rendering tiers: (a) continuous material/normal-based moss coverage and (b) optional instanced, host-anchored 3D BB shoots/cushions. A shader-only version is the minimal fallback; a pure-BB version is an experiment, not the default architecture. Avoid independent rigid-body physics for every moss BB. - **Identity:** persistent host body ID, colony ID, species ID, local attachment point and local surface normal/tangent frame, growth age, vigor, coverage radius/area, density, and deterministic seed. Do not create a unique heavyweight material per shoot or require runtime texture uploads every physics frame.
